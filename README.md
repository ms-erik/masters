# Electrical transduction of spin states of NV centers in diamond through coherent quantum transport in an antiresonant electronic structure

This repository contains the code and data used in the research paper titled "Electrical transduction of spin states of NV centers in diamond through coherent quantum transport in an antiresonant electronic structure". The study explores the mechanisms of spin manipulation in nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond, exploring antiresonant reflection electronic waveguides - ARR≡W -, analogous to the optical antiresonant reflecting optical waveguides - ARROWs.

The papers used to develop the theoretical background and experimental setup can be found in the section [Papers](./Papers).
